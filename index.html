How it gets made

  1. 1

    You pick

    Choose a design from the drop or build your own name tag above.

    Takes 1 minute
  2. 2

    We slice

    The model is cut into thin horizontal layers and turned into nozzle paths.

    0.2 mm layers · 20% infill
  3. 3

    It prints

    PLA melts at 210 °C and stacks up on a 60 °C bed. Two-colour pieces get a filament swap mid-print.

    30 to 55 minutes
  4. 4

    Ring on, out the door

    We clean the edges, fit a double split ring, test the flexi joints and pack it.

    Ships in 3 days
